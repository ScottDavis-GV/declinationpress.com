About

Why "Declination"

In navigation, declination is the angle between true north and magnetic north: the correction every navigator applies to read a compass honestly. It is also the act of saying no. We like both meanings. Declination Press is a small independent publisher based in Nevada County, in the Sierra foothills of California.
